Checkbox, Switch & Radio

Three ways to pick a value. Checkbox is on or off, or null when tristate. Switch is an instant on/off setting. Radio chooses exactly one option from a group, and the group is now managed by a RadioGroup ancestor.

Use it for

Settings, filters, terms checkboxes and single-choice questions.

Watch out for

None of them store their own value. You hold it in state and set it in onChanged. Passing null to onChanged disables the control. On Radio, groupValue and onChanged are deprecated in favour of RadioGroup.
