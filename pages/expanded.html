Expanded & Flexible

Expanded makes a child fill the space left over along the main axis of a Row, Column or Flex. Several flexible children split the free space by their flex values. Flexible is the looser version: the child may end up smaller than its share.

Use it for

Letting one child take whatever room is left, or splitting width in a ratio such as 1 : 2.

Watch out for

Expanded must be a direct child of Row, Column or Flex. Inside a scroll view running the same way there is no bounded space and Flutter throws.
