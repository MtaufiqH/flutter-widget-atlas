Painting and effects

ClipRRect, ClipOval & ClipPath

Clipping cuts the child to a shape. ClipRRect rounds the corners, ClipOval makes an ellipse, and ClipPath takes any path at all. A border radius on a decoration does not clip the child, which is why these exist.

Use it for

Rounded images and cards, circular avatars, and anything with a custom silhouette.

Watch out for

Each clip adds a layer. In a long list prefer a decoration with a radius, or a Container with clipBehavior, over an extra Clip widget.

ClipOval(child:)widget-Cuts to an ellipse that fills the box. A square child becomes a circle.
ClipPath(clipper:, child:)widget-Any shape. Write a CustomClipper<Path> and return a Path from getClip(size).
ClipPath.shape(shape:)constructor-Clip to a ShapeBorder, such as StadiumBorder, without writing a clipper.
ClipRect(child:, clipper:)widget-A plain rectangle clip. Needed to stop a child painting outside, for example with Align and a size factor.
Picking the right one
Rounded imagepattern-ClipRRect(borderRadius:, child: Image...) is the usual way. A BoxDecoration radius alone does not clip the child.
Container clipBehavioralternative-Container with a decoration and clipBehavior: Clip.antiAlias does the same in one widget.
Cardalternative-A Card already clips to its shape when you set clipBehavior.
Cost
Clipping is not freeperformance-Each clip adds a layer. In a long list, prefer a rounded image produced once, or a decoration with a border radius where you can.

Example code

ClipRRect(
  borderRadius: BorderRadius.circular(18),
  clipBehavior: Clip.antiAlias,
  child: Image.network(url, width: 200, height: 132, fit: BoxFit.cover),
