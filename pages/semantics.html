Semantics, MergeSemantics & ExcludeSemantics

The semantics tree is what TalkBack and VoiceOver read. Most widgets fill it in for you; Semantics is how you fix the cases they cannot know about, such as an icon-only button or a decorative image.

Use it for

Icon buttons, custom controls built from GestureDetector, status messages, and anything whose meaning is only visual.

Watch out for

A screen reader reads what the tree says, not what the pixels show. An icon with no label is announced as nothing at all.

const Icon(Icons.favorite, semanticLabel: 'Add to favourites')
