SafeArea

SafeArea is a Padding fed by MediaQuery: it keeps its child clear of the status bar, the notch, the home indicator and rounded corners.

Use it for

Around screen content that would otherwise sit under system furniture.

Watch out for

A Scaffold already handles the app bar and bottom bar, so wrap the body content, not the whole Scaffold. Wrapping a scroll view cuts the scrollable area, so prefer padding on the list.

What it reads
MediaQuery paddingsource-SafeArea is just a Padding fed by MediaQuery.paddingOf(context): the status bar, the home indicator, notches and rounded corners.
viewInsets vs paddingdistinction-padding is system furniture, viewInsets is the keyboard. SafeArea does not react to the keyboard unless maintainBottomViewPadding is on.
Where to put it
Inside the Scaffold bodyrule-A Scaffold already handles the app bar and bottom bar. Put SafeArea around the body content, not around the whole Scaffold.
Not around a scroll viewrule-Wrapping a list in SafeArea cuts the scroll area. Prefer padding on the list so content scrolls under the system bar.
