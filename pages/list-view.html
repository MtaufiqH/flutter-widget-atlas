Scrolling

ListView

ListView scrolls its children along one axis. The plain constructor builds every child immediately. ListView.builder creates items on demand as they scroll into view, and ListView.separated does the same while inserting a separator between items.

Use it for

Anything that scrolls. Use builder or separated for long or unknown-length lists.

Watch out for

shrinkWrap: true forces Flutter to measure every child, which removes most of the lazy-loading benefit. Prefer slivers when mixing a list with other scrolling content. A horizontal ListView inside a Column needs a fixed height, usually from a SizedBox.
