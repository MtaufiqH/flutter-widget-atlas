SizedBox & ConstrainedBox

SizedBox forces an exact size on its child. ConstrainedBox adds limits instead, so the child can still pick a size inside them. Both work by changing the constraints that reach the child.

Use it for

Fixed sizes, gaps between widgets, and minimum or maximum limits on something that sizes itself.

Watch out for

A SizedBox cannot make a child bigger than its own parent allows: the parent constraints win. An empty SizedBox is the cheapest way to add a gap.
