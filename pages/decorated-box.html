Painting and effects

DecoratedBox & BoxDecoration

BoxDecoration is what paints a box: a colour or gradient, a border, rounded corners, shadows and even a background image. DecoratedBox is the widget that draws one, behind or in front of its child.

Use it for

Any styled box. Container uses exactly this underneath.

Watch out for

color and gradient cannot both be set. With shape: BoxShape.circle you cannot set borderRadius. A rounded decoration does not clip the child: add a ClipRRect or clipBehavior.

Gradients worth knowing
LinearGradient(begin:, end:, colors:, stops:)class-A straight fade. begin and end are Alignments.
RadialGradient(center:, radius:, colors:)class-A circular fade from a point.
SweepGradient(center:, startAngle:, endAngle:, colors:)class-A fade around a circle, used for ring charts.
Other decorations
ShapeDecorationclass-Takes a ShapeBorder, so you can use StadiumBorder, CircleBorder or a custom shape. Prefer it when the shape is unusual.
UnderlineTabIndicator / BoxDecoration on TabBarclass-Decorations are also what you pass to a TabBar indicator.
Good to know
DecoratedBox vs Containerchoice-Container wraps a DecoratedBox plus padding, margin and sizing. Use DecoratedBox when you only need the painting.
Border does not cliprule-A rounded border does not cut off the child. Add a ClipRRect, or use a Container with clipBehavior.
Shadow costperformance-Large blurRadius values are expensive on older phones. Keep blur under about 24 in long lists.

BoxDecoration on api.flutter.dev

Example code

DecoratedBox(
  decoration: BoxDecoration(
    gradient: const LinearGradient(
      begin: Alignment.topLeft,
      end: Alignment.bottomRight,
      colors: [Colors.blue, Colors.red],
    ),
    borderRadius: BorderRadius.circular(18),
    border: Border.all(width: 2, color: Colors.blue),
    boxShadow: [
      BoxShadow(
        color: Colors.black.withValues(alpha: 0.32),
        blurRadius: 18,
        spreadRadius: 0,
        offset: const Offset(0, 8),
      ),
    ],
  ),
  child: const Center(child: Text('child text')),
)
