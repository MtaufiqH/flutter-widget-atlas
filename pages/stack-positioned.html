Stack & Positioned

Stack paints its children on top of each other, last one on top. Wrap a child in Positioned to pin it by distance from the edges. Children without Positioned follow the alignment, and fit decides how big they are.

Use it for

Badges, captions over images, floating buttons and any other overlap.

Watch out for

Positioned only works as a direct child of Stack. Anything outside the bounds is clipped unless clipBehavior is Clip.none. Setting left, right and width together on one axis throws.

Example code

Stack(
  alignment: AlignmentDirectional.topStart,
  clipBehavior: Clip.hardEdge,
  children: [
    Container(color: Colors.blue),
    Positioned(
      top: 12,
      right: 12,
      child: Badge(),
    ),
  ],
)
