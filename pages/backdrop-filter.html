Painting and effects

BackdropFilter

BackdropFilter filters whatever is already painted behind it. With ImageFilter.blur that gives the frosted glass look. It must sit over other content, and it must be clipped, or the blur spreads across the whole layer.

Use it for

A translucent app bar over a scrolling list, a blurred backdrop behind a sheet, a glass card over a photo.

Watch out for

It is one of the most expensive widgets in Flutter. Keep the blurred area small, never put one in a scrolling list row, and always wrap it in a clip.

ImageFilter options
ImageFilter.blur(sigmaX:, sigmaY:, tileMode:)class-Gaussian blur. Sigma 8 to 20 reads as frosted glass.
ImageFilter.matrix(Matrix4.storage)class-Scale, rotate or skew what is behind.
ImageFilter.compose(outer:, inner:)class-Chain two filters.
Rules that catch people out
Needs something behind itrule-BackdropFilter blurs what is already painted, so it must sit over other content, normally inside a Stack.
Always clip itrule-The blur spreads to the whole layer. Wrap it in a ClipRRect or ClipRect, or the whole screen goes soft.
Costperformance-It is one of the most expensive widgets in Flutter. Keep the area small and avoid it inside scrolling lists.
Where it is used
Frosted app barpattern-A translucent AppBar over a scrolling list, with extendBodyBehindAppBar: true on the Scaffold.
Dialog backgroundpattern-Blur the page behind a dialog or a bottom sheet.
ImageFilteredwidget-Blurs the child itself rather than the background, and is cheaper.

Example code

import 'dart:ui';

ClipRRect(
  borderRadius: BorderRadius.circular(18),
  child: BackdropFilter(
    filter: ImageFilter.blur(sigmaX: 10, sigmaY: 10),
    child: Container(
      color: Colors.white.withValues(alpha: 0.25),
      child: content,
    ),
  ),
)
